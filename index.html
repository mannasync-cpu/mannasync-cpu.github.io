About

I am a medical professional (MBBS) with clinical research coordination experience in Phase II/III neurology trials, specializing in cognitive neurology assessments. I have administered and scored standardized psychometric assessments including ADAS-COG, mRS, MoCA, EQ-5D-5L, ADCS-ADL, and ADCS-CGIC across 10 neurology studies covering Alzheimer's Disease, Parkinson's Disease, Multiple Sclerosis, and Stroke.

I am also the Founder & CEO of MannaSync Solutions Inc., a health-technology company building AI-powered platforms for healthcare delivery. I am currently seeking clinical research opportunities while preparing for US medical residency.
